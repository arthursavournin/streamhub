Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026 · English translation; the French version prevails.

In short

  • Without an account (guest), StreamHub collects no personal data: everything stays in your browser or on your device.
  • With an account, StreamHub keeps your email address and a copy of your settings and lists, end-to-end encrypted: neither the publisher nor the host can read it.
  • No ads, no tracking, no audience measurement, no selling of data.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time: Settings → Account → Delete my account.

1. Data controller

Arthur Savournin, publisher of StreamHub on a non-professional basis.
Contact for any question about your data: streamhub.projet@gmail.com.

2. Without an account: nothing leaves your device

Settings, API keys, subscriptions, Likes, My list, history and downloads are saved in your browser’s local storage (localStorage) or in the app. The publisher has no access to them. This storage is strictly necessary for the service you request, so it does not require consent (article 82 of the French Data Protection Act). StreamHub uses no advertising or audience-measurement cookies. You can erase everything by deleting the site’s data in your browser.

3. With an account

Data processed

  • Email address, account creation and last sign-in dates.
  • Password: your real password never leaves your device. StreamHub derives another secret from it, which Supabase keeps as a (non-reversible) hash.
  • Sessions: IP address, browser or device type and dates, to keep you signed in and protect your session.
  • Vault and synced data: settings, API keys, subscriptions, home page, Likes, My list, history and likes on platforms, encrypted on your device (AES-256-GCM) with a key that only your password and your recovery key can open. The host only sees unreadable content, its size and the date of its last change.

Why and on what basis

To create your account, sign you in and sync your data across your devices, i.e. to provide the service set out in the terms of use (performance of a contract, article 6.1.b GDPR). Session information is also used for the security of the service (legitimate interest, article 6.1.f).

How long

  • Account and synced data: until you delete your account. An account with no activity at all (sign-in or sync) for 3 years is deleted automatically.
  • Sign-up never confirmed: deleted after 7 days.
  • Sessions: until sign-out, and at most 90 days after their last use.
  • Supabase technical logs (requests, IP addresses): 1 day.

4. Emails

Your email address is only used to send you the messages the account needs: address confirmation and forgotten password. They are sent through Mailjet (Sinch group). No newsletter, no commercial messages.

5. Signing in with Google or GitHub

If you choose “Continue with Google” or “Continue with GitHub”, that service sends StreamHub your email address, your name and the address of your profile picture. StreamHub only uses the email address, to identify your account; the name and picture are kept as sent by the sign-in service but are not shown, used or shared. StreamHub does not request access to any other data of your Google or GitHub account (no emails, contacts or files), publishes nothing on your behalf and passes this information to no one. Your synced data stays encrypted with your vault password, which Google and GitHub do not know.

6. How the site works

  • Site hosting: GitHub Pages records visitors’ IP addresses for the security of its service.
  • StreamHub relay (Cloudflare): to find links to platforms, your browser sends it the identifier and title of the movie or series. The relay only keeps these answers (a few hours), with no link to you.
  • Services called directly by your browser: TMDB (catalog and images), Streaming Availability (with your key, if you entered one), Wikidata (search by nickname) and, when you choose to go there, the streaming platforms’ websites. They receive your IP address and what you request, and apply their own privacy rules. Your API keys are only sent to the service concerned.
  • Password check: when you choose a password, your device checks that it does not appear in known data breaches (Have I Been Pwned service). Only the first 5 characters of a hash of the password are sent, never the password itself or your email address.
  • “Resume after sign-in” script (optional): it keeps the title’s link in your browser, on the platform’s site, while you sign in. It sends nothing to StreamHub.

8. Security

Encrypted connections (HTTPS), end-to-end encrypted synced data, access rules that limit each account to its own data. Keep your password and recovery key to yourself: without them, nobody, not even the publisher, can reopen your synced data.

9. Your rights

You can ask to access your data, correct it, erase it, restrict or object to its processing, and get your data back (portability). The simplest way:

  • Erase: Settings → Account → Delete my account (immediate erasure of the account, the vault and the synced data).
  • View or get back: your data can be read in StreamHub on your devices; since it is encrypted, the publisher cannot read it for you.
  • For any other request: streamhub.projet@gmail.com. Answer within one month at most.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can file a complaint with the CNIL, the French data protection authority (cnil.fr, 3 place de Fontenoy, TSA 80715, 75334 Paris Cedex 07, France).

10. Minors

You must be at least 15 years old to create an account, or have a parent’s consent under 15 (article 45 of the French Data Protection Act).

11. Changes

This policy may change along with StreamHub. The date of the last update is shown at the top of the page; in case of a significant change, StreamHub will let you know.
